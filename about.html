About Me

I’m Prottoy Roy, a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at AIUB (CGPA 3.99/4.00). I’m interested in AI and machine learning, with hands-on work in time-series forecasting, NLP, computer vision, and explainable models. Relevant coursework includes Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Algorithms, Artificial Intelligence and Expert Systems, Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition, Data Warehouse and Data Mining, and Basic Graph Theory.

I work with Python, TensorFlow/Keras, scikit-learn, Hugging Face Transformers, OpenCV, pandas, and NumPy. I also use LLM APIs, LangChain, and prompt engineering, with basic REST API deployment in FastAPI and Docker. Other tools include SQL, C++, Java, C#, JavaScript, PHP, Git, and MySQL. I speak Bengali natively and English fluently.

Two of my research papers were accepted in 2026: work on explainable dengue outbreak forecasting and FloodNet-VQA for disaster assessment. I’m seeking an AI/ML engineering role where I can build, evaluate, and explain models on real data.
